I am a PhD student in the DATAMOVE research team at Laboratoire d'Informatique de Grenoble (LIG), Université Grenoble Alpes. My research focuses on evaluating and mitigating the environmental footprint and energy consumption of Large Language Models (LLMs) across cloud and high-performance computing (HPC) infrastructures.

Holding a Master's degree from ENSIMAG (MoSiG, Data Science & AI) and a Magister in Computer Science from Université Grenoble Alpes, my core technical and research interests span neural architectures (Transformers), parallel computing (CUDA/GPU), and sustainable, eco-responsible AI.
