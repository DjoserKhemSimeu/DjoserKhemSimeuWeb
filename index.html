PhD Student in Computer Science • Inria / DATAMOVE Team • Université Grenoble Alpes

I am a PhD student in the DATAMOVE research team at Inria (joint team with LIG / Université Grenoble Alpes). My research focuses on evaluating and mitigating the environmental footprint and energy consumption of Large Language Models (LLMs) across cloud and high-performance computing (HPC) infrastructures.

Holding a dual qualification with a Master's degree from ENSIMAG (MoSiG, Data Science & AI) and a concurrent Magister degree in Computer Science from Université Grenoble Alpes, my core technical and research interests span neural architectures (Transformers), parallel computing (CUDA/GPU), and sustainable, frugal AI.
